## 3. Ecosystem & Compatibility
- Wi-Fi devices generally require a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network for initial pairing. A 5GHz-only network is not supported by current Wi-Fi products.
- Glow lighting products can be grouped into rooms and scenes through NOVAHAUS Home.
- Sense Door, Sense Motion, and Sense Temp use Zigbee and require Home Hub Mini or Home Hub Pro.
- Home Hub Pro provides the broadest ecosystem integration and can expose compatible devices to Apple Home.
- Google Home and Amazon Alexa integration requires the customer's NOVAHAUS account to be linked to the relevant third-party platform.
- Cloud-dependent features may be unavailable during an internet outage; supported local automations can continue through a Home Hub.
- Product compatibility can vary by firmware version. Customers should keep NOVAHAUS Home and device firmware updated.

## 4. Installation & Safety Notes
- Do not install smart switches while mains power is live. Installation of fixed electrical products should be performed by a qualified electrician.
- Connect Switch 1G and 2G require a neutral wire.
- Connect Plug Mini is rated for loads up to 10A; Connect Plug Power is rated for loads up to 16A. Do not exceed the rated load.
- Keep indoor cameras and sensors away from direct water exposure.
- Secure Doorbell is rated IP54 and is intended for sheltered outdoor locations rather than direct high-pressure water exposure.
- Do not use a damaged cable, adapter, plug, or enclosure.
- Do not open or modify mains-powered devices.
- Battery-powered sensors should use the specified battery type and polarity.
