## 10. Troubleshooting

**Q: My smart bulb will not connect. What should I try first?**
A: Confirm the bulb is powered, connect the phone to 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, move closer to the router, reset the bulb, and retry pairing.

**Q: My camera is offline. What should I check?**
A: Check power, Wi-Fi signal, router status, internet availability, and whether the device LED indicates normal operation.

**Q: My sensor is not responding. What should I check?**
A: Check battery level, hub connectivity, Zigbee coverage, and whether the sensor is still registered in NOVAHAUS Home.

**Q: My smart plug stopped responding after a router change. Why?**
A: A changed Wi-Fi SSID or password can require the plug to be paired again.

**Q: My automation does not run. What should I check?**
A: Verify that the trigger device is online, the automation is enabled, the time/conditions are correct, and any required Home Hub is online.

**Q: Why is my device slow to respond?**
A: Network congestion, weak signal, internet latency, or cloud-service conditions can affect response time.

**Q: Should I reset the device immediately?**
A: Try basic power, network, and connectivity checks first. Resetting a device may erase its existing configuration.
