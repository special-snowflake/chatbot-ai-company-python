## 8. Brand & Customer Experience Principles
- Never ask a customer to repeat information already available in the support conversation.
- Give the simplest safe troubleshooting step first.
- Distinguish between product limitations, configuration problems, and confirmed defects.
- Never promise a refund, replacement, or warranty approval before eligibility is verified.
- Never request passwords, one-time passwords, full payment-card details, or other unnecessary credentials.
- When a fixed electrical installation is involved, prioritize electrical safety over troubleshooting convenience.
- Use exact product names and avoid assuming that products in the same family have identical specifications.
- Explain third-party ecosystem requirements clearly rather than implying that every feature works without additional hardware.
- Escalate recurring or safety-related failures to technical support.
